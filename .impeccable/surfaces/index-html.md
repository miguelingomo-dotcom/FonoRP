# Surface brief: FonoRP app (index.html)

Scope: the whole app (inicio, ejercicio, corrección, modo papel, resumen) plus the brand identity (logo, palette, PWA icons). Mode: Operate. Audience and task: see PRODUCT.md; one word at a time on an iPhone, 5 or 10 words per round.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each word is an exam exercise and each correction is the examining board's stamp. Refuses the category default of a white flashcard with green/red toasts.

OWN-WORLD: Spanish oposición paperwork. Cool "papel de oficio" ground, ink-black 1.5px form rules, sharp boxes (radius 2-4px), field labels set small in Archivo condensed, IPA in Noto Sans. Violet tampón ink is the brand and the "APTO" stamp; corrector red is the error pen and the "REVISAR" stamp. Phoneme chart on a dark ink tray in flat colour zones: vowels blue-grey, diphthongs lavender, consonants paper, extras dashed.

STORY: The user sees an exercise form, fills the transcription casilla by casilla, hands it in, and the board stamps it; red pen marks show exactly which casilla failed and why.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top: form header strip (Ejercicio nn/nn with tick boxes, Nivel, patrones). Below: the word in a labelled "Palabra" field at display size. Then the transcription as a row of casillas (marks in narrow dashed boxes). Bottom: the chart tray with the primary action at bottom right. Signature move: on Comprobar a rubber stamp lands over the answer row (scale settles, slight rotation, ink opacity) and the chart lights the word's sounds.

FORM: Grounded candidate 5 of 7 (hoja de examen de oposición). seed:f92eada8f2c4c26b. Raises: state by form as well as hue (strike, dashed gap, doubled primary stress); flat colour fields per chart zone; user answer and model registered on one baseline.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
